Design and backtest strategies whose goal is to turn $100,000 into $300,000 within 12 months, trading only AMD, or AMD plus one other US stock from: NVDA MSFT AAPL GOOGL AMZN META AVGO TSLA LLY V XOM MA JNJ PLTR CVX CSCO INTC MU ISRG NOW QCOM TXN.

Hard limits: long-only, cash account, no margin, no leverage, no shorting, no options, no leveraged ETFs. Backtest only. Never connect to a broker or place an order.

Method:
1. Use your own backtest tool with source yfinance, codes like AMD.US, daily bars, initial_cash 100000.
2. Develop only on 2015-01-01 to 2022-12-31. Try at least six genuinely different ideas, such as trend, breakout, pullback, volatility regime, relative-strength rotation between two names, and earnings or seasonal effects.
3. Pick your best two from the development period only, then run them once, unchanged, on 2023-01-01 to 2023-12-31, 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31, 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 and 2025-11-03 to 2026-11-03.
4. Report a table with the multiple on starting capital and max drawdown per period, next to buy-and-hold of the same names.
5. Say plainly whether any out-of-sample period reached 3x, and whether that came from the strategy or from the stock simply rising.

Finish by printing the exact rules and parameters of each strategy you tried, so they can be re-run in another engine.
