## CLAIM
The inside bar as a compression-then-expansion trigger.

## EXACT MECHANICS
An **inside bar** is a bar whose high is lower and whose low is higher
than the previous bar's -- the whole range sits inside its
predecessor. The trade is the break of the inside bar's high, with
the stop at its low; the pattern is read as a volatility contraction
that resolves in the direction of the break.

## WHAT NEEDS FUTURE INFORMATION
Nothing. It is a two-bar definition on completed bars.
