## CLAIM
Five 'non-negotiable rules'.

## EXACT MECHANICS
1. The general market must be in an uptrend (10/20 EMA crossover).
2. The stock must be above a **rising 50-DAY moving average**.
3. A prior move / relative strength.
4. Good liquidity.
5. A clear stop-loss level.
Entries: pullbacks to the 10-day or 21-day EMA, weekly 20 EMA.

## WHAT NEEDS FUTURE INFORMATION
Nothing needs the future -- but the timeframe does: **every moving
average here is DAILY or WEEKLY, so this is a SWING strategy held for
days.** It is out of scope for a same-day, flat-by-the-close mandate.

## HALAL
Long-only; would be compatible if the holding period were allowed.
