## EXACT MECHANICS
- Opening range = **the first 15 minutes**.
- Entry when a candle **CLOSES** beyond the range (not a stop-buy
  through it) -- 'once this candle closes, that is going to be our
  entry'.
- Stop 'right below where the previous [candle low] is'; he notes that
  when price is very close to the level an 'ideal stop' is wider.
- Supply/demand zones and the high of day are used as targets.
