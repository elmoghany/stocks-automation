## CLAIM
Anchored VWAP as a single decisive tool.

## EXACT MECHANICS
- Anchor a VWAP at a chosen event -- the prior day's low/high, a gap
  day, an earnings print -- rather than at 09:30.
- 'The rules are pretty simple: identify the [anchor]'; then trade
  price's reaction to that line. 'Our stop loss should be above the
  VWAP... we can target the recent lows.'

## WHAT NEEDS FUTURE INFORMATION
The ANCHOR CHOICE is the entire strategy and it is discretionary --
'a significant event' is judged by the trader. A causal, mechanical
anchor does exist (the session's own low so far, or the prior day's
close) and would be testable, but it is a DIFFERENT rule from the one
taught. **UNTESTABLE-AS-STATED; the mechanical-anchor version is
QUEUED**, not claimed as the video's rule.
