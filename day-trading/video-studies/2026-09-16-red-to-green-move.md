## EXACT MECHANICS
A stock opens BELOW the prior day's close (red on the day) and then
crosses ABOVE it -- buy the cross of the prior close, stop below the
low of the crossing candle, ride the momentum that the flip attracts.
(The mirror, green-to-red, is a short and is dropped.)
