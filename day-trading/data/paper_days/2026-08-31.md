### Veto-counting convention adopted today
A veto **decision** is counted when (a) a fresh Trigger C prints, or (b) the ratchet level **moves**. Re-checking an unchanged level is *monitoring*, not a new decision. Without this the rate would measure polling frequency rather than the strategy's refusal rate.

**Post-open rate: 4 vetoes / 6 decisions = 66.7%**, just above the modelled 50–65% band. Premarket: 1/1 = 100%, on a single decision.

## 5. Findings

**1. Warrants were never being excluded — fixed in-session.**
`EOSEW` ("Eos Energy Enterprises, Inc. Warrant") crossed +18.18% and reached the candidate pool. Neither `FUND_PAT` nor `SPAC_PAT` catches a warrant, yet C37 eligibility requires **common stock**. Two failures, not one: it was never tradeable, *and* the ranker would have mis-measured it anyway — a warrant's prev_close, coil and premarket high are derivative of the underlying's tape, so it would have competed for TOP on numbers that mean something different. Added `NONCOMMON_PAT` to `scan_sweep.py` (warrants, rights, units, preferreds, debentures) with its own drop bucket; verified on the 06:31 dump, candidates 15 → 14. Fixed **in the tool, not in my attention** — remembering to exclude it every cycle is exactly the hand-ranking drift the `rank` command exists to prevent.

**2. The scanner's `Last` is a stale mark for premarket-dark names.**
Four names entered the crossed set on crosses that **never printed**:

| Sym | Scan says | Real tape | Truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTW | 2.90 = +12.40% | no trades, flat 2.55 | **−1.2%, the name is DOWN** |

| EDRY | 57.15 = +13.45% | 155 sh @ 53.00 | +5.2% |
| CVR | 12.00 = +17.19% | zero trades | never crossed |

The latch exists because the champion treats a +10% cross as permanent **once printed**. This inflates the live universe invisibly — the mirror of parity-audit divergence #2. Cost nothing today (all four were excluded as NO BARS), and the ranker later confirmed it independently, refusing EDRY with *"+10% cross has NOT printed yet"*. **Deliberately not purged** — dropping a latched name is under-discovery, and a real print later makes them genuinely eligible. Recommended fix (gate the latch on a real print) belongs in a reviewed patch, not a mid-session edit.

**3. gap7 completion attempted on MOVE and correctly NOT applied.**
MOVE (Corvex, $316M AI/GPU cloud) ranked **first** on coil 0.926 and the best pressure of the day (+0.35), halal PASS on both questions — and read CALM-GAP FAIL from a missing 7AM bar. Per standing instruction the gap was chased on Polygon. Polygon returned **zero** bars for MOVE while returning full tapes for SPY and NEOV controls. The real explanation: **MOVE's first print of the day was 07:45 ET.** There was no 7AM bar to complete because the stock had not started trading. CALM-GAP FAIL stands and is *real*, not a data artefact. Because gap7 is anchored to a fixed clock time, MOVE was **structurally unarmable for the entire session** no matter how it traded — flagged as a genuine question for review.

Without the controls, MOVE's empty result would have read as a broken feed or as evidence the stock was dark all morning.
