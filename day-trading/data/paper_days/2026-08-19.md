### Process lessons (recorded in JSON too)

1. **Delegation latency vs Trigger C**: the detect→handback→re-quote loop is
   ~3 min against a 2-min freshness window. Pattern entries are only takeable
   by whoever holds arming authority in a ≤1-min loop. Resting stops are the
   protocol-native answer (and worked: the day's only fill).
2. **Background-timer delegation stalls** — position watches must pace with
   foreground waits; state-file mtime is the dead-man check (caught in 9 min).
3. Ranking flip-flop within the coil group (pressure noise) should not
   re-pick every 5 minutes; pick continuity + the 10:00 stale-pick escape is
   the stable reading of C37.
4. MRVL held its −3–6% band for six hours: the no-profit-take rule kept the
   full loss to the flatten, and the trail (anchored to a 3-min-old peak)
   never tightened usefully. This is the champion's designed give-back —
   logged as data, not a proposed change.
