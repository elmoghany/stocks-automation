## 7. Ops defects found and fixed in-session

**(a) HIGH — cross-day dump collision injected four of yesterday's symbols.**
Dumps were named `scan_dump_{HHMM}.json` with no date. At 07:24 the delta build
correctly refused and wrote nothing, so the sweep read the *pre-existing*
`scan_dump_0724.json` — Day 19's tape — and reported YDDL/NEOV/ANPA/DCOY as NEW
crossers while marking all twelve of today's real candidates GONE. **Not
self-correcting:** the crossed set is a latch, so four names whose +10% cross never
printed today would have sat in the pool until 14:30. NEOV was yesterday's actual
traded name and would have looked entirely plausible on a re-rank.
*Caught* because the sweep printed `rows=18/18` against a live scan of 73, with
`dropped funds=0` — a scan that is 95% ETFs cannot have no funds in it.
*Fixed:* phantoms purged with reason recorded inside `scan_state`; filenames
date-scoped; and `scan_sweep.py` now **refuses any dump whose mtime date differs
from the date being swept**, which catches this independently of naming. Verified
both directions.

**(b) Clock drift — the session was estimating ET from tool-call count** and had
drifted ~45 min *ahead* of real time (believed 07:16 at 06:30). Harmless causally,
but dangerous near the 14:30 cutoff. Fixed with `plan/wait_until.py`, which makes
every wait clock-driven, auditable, and hard-capped at 480 s so none can approach
the 600 s background-and-die threshold.

**(c) The scan returned a CRYPTO row.** UNI "Uniswap" at +11.95%. All three existing
drop patterns match on *Name*, and no regex will ever catch a coin. The tool guide
warns that `instrument_type` is useless for screening because EQUITY covers stocks
and funds alike — true, but one-directional: a **non-EQUITY value is decisive**.
`scan_sweep` now drops those. `dump_from_delta` was fixed in the same breath — it
hardcoded `EQUITY` on every row it wrote and would have walked the coin past the new
guard. Fourth contamination class in four sessions (leveraged ETFs, SPACs, warrants,
now crypto); root cause is that the saved scan filters on price and %change only.

**(d) yfinance 404s on dot-class tickers.** HVT.A read `NO FUNDAMENTALS DATA` — a
*refusal to evaluate* indistinguishable from a compliance FAIL in the ledger.
`live_halal.py` now falls back to the dash form then the root, and logs the
substitution. HVT.A then resolved to a genuine FAIL at combined 78.7%.
